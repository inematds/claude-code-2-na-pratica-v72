Claude Code 2.0 in Practice: New Features That Change Your Day

Claude Code added side chat, split screen, helpers, phone control, cloud work, its own browser, mods, and more. In seven short lessons, you’ll try each feature on a real task with a ready-to-use prompt, and see what you can use today. Duet and Motion are introduced, not tested.

The lessons

From an overview of the new features to making them part of your routine: organizing, delegating, using apps and sites, working away from your computer, sharing work with others, and customizing.

  1. Lesson 1 · 14 minThe new features map: what changed and what you already haveMany people talk about "Claude Code 2.0," but few explain what actually changed or what you can use today. In this lesson, you’ll see the 13 new features in six groups, learn what each one requires (app, plan, version), and check in your own Claude what you already have, what you don’t, and what’s on the waitlist.
  2. Lesson 2 · 15 minOrganize: one place per project and questions on the sideA conversation full of unrelated questions gets slow and confusing, and a huge list of conversations hides the work. Three new features fix that: project sections in the sidebar, side chat for quick questions, and split view to see two conversations side by side. In this lesson, Carla organizes her spreadsheet course using all three.
  3. Lesson 3 · 15 minHelpers: one big job split into partsA finished script can become a summary, clips, and posts. Asking for everything in one conversation fills it up and mixes the parts together. With helpers, the conversation splits up the work: each helper handles one part and returns only the result. In this lesson, Carla does this with lesson 1 of the spreadsheet course, and you try it with a text of your own.
  4. Lesson 4 · 16 minApps and websites: Claude uses them for you, following your rulesNot every program or website has an easy way to automate it. Now Claude can use an app on your computer (computer use) or a website through the browser built into the app. Being able to do something doesn’t mean you should do everything. In this lesson, you’ll allow only what’s needed and run a read-only task on one of your websites, checking that nothing changed.
  5. Lesson 5 · 15 minAway from your computer: approve on your phone and send work to the cloudBig projects don’t have to keep you in your chair. With Remote Control, you can follow a session running on your computer from your phone. With Projects, research runs in the cloud, even with your laptop closed. In this lesson, Carla approves a plan from the couch and sends research to the cloud, knowing what leaves her computer.
  6. Lesson 6 · 15 minDeliver: slides, dashboards, and shared documentsFinished work only matters when it reaches someone. Now you can ask Claude for a presentation, graphic, numbers dashboard, or document, see it finished in the conversation, make changes in plain language, and share it by link. In this lesson, Carla turns lesson 1 into 7 slides and creates a guest document the team can edit together, checking it before sending.
  7. Lesson 7 · 16 minCustomize: slash commands, mods, and your routineFive short commands change how Claude works: undo, see what’s taking up space in the conversation, summarize, work toward a goal, and give shorter answers. Mods change how Claude Code looks, but you should check them first. At the end, Carla picks three new things from the course and writes her routine for the week. You’ll make yours.

Who it’s for

  1. 1People who create content, teach, or run a small business and already use Claude.
  2. 2People who have heard of “Claude Code 2.0” and want to know what really changed.
  3. 3People who don’t code and want to use Claude Code with ready-to-use prompts.

What you’ll be able to do

  1. 1Organize projects into sections and ask questions in a side chat without cluttering the conversation.
  2. 2Split a big task among helpers.
  3. 3Let Claude use apps and websites for you, safely.
  4. 4Follow along on your phone and send research to the cloud.
  5. 5Share slides, dashboards, and documents.
  6. 6Customize Claude with slash commands and mods.

Where to use it

  1. 1In the Claude app for Mac or Windows (Code tab).
  2. 2Terminal only if you want (optional): the entire course works in the app.
  3. 3Paid plan: Pro, Max, or Ultra. Some features are in testing; each lesson will let you know.
