Lesson 6 · 15 min

Deliver: slides, dashboards, and shared documents

Finished work only matters when it reaches someone. Now you can ask Claude for a presentation, graphic, numbers dashboard, or document, see it finished in the conversation, make changes in plain language, and share it by link. In this lesson, Carla turns lesson 1 into 7 slides and creates a guest document the team can edit together, checking it before sending.

Start the lesson

In this lesson, you’ll

  1. 1The deliverable types and which plan supports each one
  2. 2Adjust the piece with words, without another program
  3. 3Check 3 numbers or dates before sharing
  4. 4Share with view, comment, or edit access

Goal: Turn the work into slides, a dashboard, or a shared document, and check it before sending.

01 · From result to link

Tap or click each step. Check it before sharing the link, never after.

Ask

Scroll down: each step below lights up its part of the diagram.

  1. 1/6

    Ask

    Say what you want, where the content comes from, and what it should look like: how many slides, what colors, who will read it. The clearer your request, the fewer back-and-forths later.

  2. 2/6

    View

    The piece appears right in the conversation, without opening another program. Look at it as your student or client will: can they understand it in a few seconds?

  3. 3/6

    Adjust

    You make changes by saying things like: "make the text bigger," "remove slide 4," "replace the chart with a table." One change at a time is easier to check.

  4. 4/6

    Check

    Choose 3 numbers or dates in the piece and compare them with the original source: the spreadsheet, calendar, or script. Claude can get a number wrong with complete confidence. If one of the three is wrong, check them all.

  5. 5/6

    Share

    The piece starts out private: only you can see it. In the Share button, you choose who can access it. In a document, each person can view, comment, or edit. As the doc owner, you can tag @Claude in a comment so it can review the document right there.

  6. 6/6

    Checked and ready to share

    Describe the piece in words, adjust it in words, check 3 numbers against the source, and only then share the link.

Asking, viewing, and adjusting can take a few rounds. Checking and sharing happen once, in that order.

02 · What you can ask for

The official names and which plans include each one. There is no "Motion Studio": the correct name is Claude Motion.

1. Slides and Design

Claude Slides makes presentations; Claude Design makes graphics, like a post cover or a poster.

Both are available on all plans. They’re the best place to start.

Carla7 slides for lesson 1 and a graphic to promote the lesson in the channel.

2. Dashboards

A dashboard is a panel of numbers: charts and totals on one screen, built from a spreadsheet or data you provide.

It’s in beta (testing). Before showing it to anyone, check 3 numbers against the source.

CarlaA dashboard showing course sign-ups by month, built from her spreadsheet.

3. Claude Motion

Claude Motion creates animated videos with code and exports them as MP4 files.

It’s in beta and available only on Team and Enterprise plans. It doesn’t appear on Pro, Max, or Ultra, so stick with Slides and Design.

4. Claude Docs

Claude creates a document you can share by link. Everyone works on the same copy, with no versions lost in email.

Anyone who opens it needs to sign in with a Claude account. You choose who can view, comment, or edit.

CarlaA list of guests for this month’s livestreams, updated by the moderator.

03 · Carla’s prompts: slides, dashboard, and artwork

Works on claude.ai and in the app. Send the script with your request or mention the file if you’re in the Code tab; replace spreadsheet-course/… with the path to your file (.md, Word, or PDF; see lesson 2).

1 · Slide request
Create a 7-slide presentation from the lesson 1 script (spreadsheet-course/lesson-1/script.md).
Audience: adults who have never used formulas.
One idea per slide, no more than 20 words per slide, large text.
Colors: black and yellow.
Slide 7: an exercise for the student to do on their own.
2 · Edits in plain English
Slide 3: replace the text with a spreadsheet example with 4 rows.
Slide 5: larger text and fewer words.
Don’t change the other slides.
3 · Fact-check request
List all numbers, dates, and formulas that appear in the 7 slides.
For each one, say which line of the script it’s on.
Mark anything that isn’t in the script with ATTENTION.
4 · Numbers dashboard (Dashboards, beta)
Create a dashboard from this spreadsheet: <attach the spreadsheet or give the file name>.
Show subscribers by month in a chart and highlight the period total.
Use only the numbers in the spreadsheet. Don’t make any up.
5 · An image (Claude Design)
Create a square image to promote <what you want to promote> on my channel.
Short title, large text, black and yellow colors.
No more than 12 words in the image.
Simulation: illustrative example
  1. A spreadsheet isn’t rocket science.
  2. Cell, row, and column: the address of everything.
  3. A shopping list with 4 items.
  4. The first formula: =SUM(B2:B5).
  5. When the sum is wrong: a cell with text.
  6. Copy the formula down.
  7. Exercise: add up the week’s expenses.
  • =SUM(B2:B5) (slide 4): script, line 18.
  • 4 items (slide 3): script, line 12.
  • ATTENTION: "7 days" (slide 7): it isn’t in the script; I added it. Want to keep it?
  • Ask for everything again because of one slide: request an edit to just that slide.
  • Send it without checking the numbers: a pretty slide with the wrong formula makes for a wrong lesson.

04 · Carla’s prompt: the guest document

A Claude Doc the team can edit together.

Shared document
Create a document to track guests for my community’s livestreams in December.
A table with: name, livestream topic, date, time, and status (invited, confirmed, recorded).
Start with 3 sample rows marked EXAMPLE.
Below the table, add a short list of what to do during the week of the live stream.
Make it ready to share with my assistant.
Simulation: illustrative example
  1. Click Share on the document.
  2. Choose access, for example Only you or Anyone with the link, plus an email invitation. Options vary by plan.
  3. For your assistant: send an email invitation with edit permission.
  4. Test it: send the link to someone on your team and ask them to open it. They’ll sign in with their Claude account.

Carla, the doc owner, comments on a livestream row:

"@Claude, check whether any date falls on a December holiday and let me know here."

Claude replies in the comment itself, so Carla doesn’t need to start another conversation.

  • The document starts out private; you decide whether to share it.
  • Anyone who opens it needs a Claude account.
  • With "Anyone with the link," whoever has the link can open it, so share it only with people you trust. If the document uses Claude inside it or connects to your apps, this option isn’t available.

05 · Practice: checked deliverables

All in claude.ai or the app. No terminal needed.

E1 · The slides

20 min

Ask for 7 slides based on one of your scripts, lessons, or texts, using Carla’s template. Make at least one change by typing a request. Then run the fact-check request.

Done when: the presentation has 7 slides, you made 1 change by typing a request, and you noted 3 numbers, dates, or formulas checked against the original text.

E2 · The shared document

15 min

Create a tracking document (guests, deliverables, or weekly tasks) using Carla’s adapted prompt, and share it with someone on your team using the Share button. Choose view, comment, or edit access. They’ll need to sign in with a Claude account to open it.

Done when: you replaced the 3 EXAMPLE rows with 3 real livestreams (or tasks), checked the dates against your calendar, sent the link to someone on your team, and they opened it (working alone? you shared it and confirmed the share setting shows the access you chose, such as view).

E3 · A request to @Claude

5 min

In your document, leave a comment tagging @Claude with a request to review one row of the table. Only you, the document owner, can call on @Claude; the people you shared it with can view, comment, or edit, depending on the access you gave each one.

Done when: Claude’s reply appears in the comment itself.

E4 · A dashboard (Dashboards, beta)

10 min

Dashboards is in beta (testing): if you don’t see it, skip this one. Get a simple spreadsheet with about 10 numbers (subscribers, sales, or expenses by month), and paste an adapted version of the "Numbers dashboard" prompt.

Done when: the dashboard appears in the conversation and you checked 2 of its numbers against the spreadsheet.

E5 · An image (Design)

10 min

Paste the "An image" prompt with what you want to promote. Make one change by typing a request (color, text size, or wording).

Done when: the image appears in the conversation, you made 1 change by typing a request, and you read the text in the image without finding any errors.
